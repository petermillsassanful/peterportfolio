The Zenrixa Vision platform is more than a headset. It's a complete ecosystem for creating, deploying, and scaling immersive experiences.

8K Micro-OLED Display
Per-eye 4K resolution with HDR support and 120Hz refresh. Colors so accurate you forget you're wearing a headset.
Ultra-Low Latency
7ms motion-to-photon latency achieved through our proprietary rendering pipeline. No nausea. No lag. Just presence.
AI Content Generation
Built-in AI tools that generate spatial scenes, 3D assets, and animations from natural language prompts in seconds.
Spatial Audio Engine
Binaural 360° audio with head-tracking and real-time room acoustics. You hear where you look.
Multi-user Collaboration
Up to 50 simultaneous users in a shared spatial environment. Persistent worlds, real-time sync, full body avatars.
Enterprise Security
End-to-end encrypted sessions, SSO integration, and granular permissions. SOC 2 Type II compliant.
